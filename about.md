<p>
BangPypers, the Bangalore Python Usergroup is one of the largest and oldest Python user groups in India.
</p>

<p>
The group maintains a <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://mail.python.org/mailman3/lists/bangpypers.python.org/">mailing list</a> to discuss about various python-related topics and organizes monthly meetups regularly. Meetups usually happen on the third Saturday of every month. Look at our <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://meetup.com/BangPypers/">Meetup.com page</a> for upcoming meetups.
</p>
<p>
<strong>Every BangPypers meetup is free.</strong> If Meetup.com asks you to pay when you RSVP, choose “Continue without paying”.
</p>
<p>
Between meetups we run <a href="{{ '/#paper-n-pip' | relative_url }}">Paper-n-PiP</a>, an online session on Discord where a member walks through a research paper or a Python topic. Recordings are in the <a href="{{ site.data.paper_n_pip.playlist }}">Paper-n-PiP playlist on YouTube</a>. To present a session, submit your talk in the <a href="https://github.com/bangpypers/meetup-talks">meetup-talks repository</a>.
</p>
<p>
Volunteers run every meetup: they find venues, line up speakers and look after the day itself. Read <a href="{% post_url 2017-06-03-how-bangpypers-is-run %}">how BangPypers is run</a> to see what goes into it.
</p>
