### For companies

Hiring Python developers? Use the form below to submit your company's job openings
for BangPypers volunteers to review. This form is only for companies posting jobs.

Job submissions are not published on this website. Public listings and which
details may be shared are still under discussion.

### Looking for a job?

Connect with a BangPypers volunteer at one of our
[meetups](https://www.meetup.com/BangPypers/). Volunteers can introduce you to
companies offline.
