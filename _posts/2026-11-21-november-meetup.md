The [November BangPypers](https://www.meetup.com/bangpypers/events/312819340/) meetup was held at the [Automation Anywhere](https://www.linkedin.com/company/automation-anywhere) office on Palace Road, Bengaluru, in collaboration with the [Pathfinder Community](https://community.automationanywhere.com/bengaluru-chapter-85088). The day was all about AI and robotics, with talks covering expressive robots, production-ready MCP tools and robot simulation from natural language.

[Kevin Rohan Vaz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevin-rohan-vaz/) kicked off the meetup with *Building an Expressive AI on the Reachy Mini*, showing how to give a small open-source robot an AI-driven personality using Python.

![Building an Expressive AI on the Reachy Mini](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/c/f/f/d/highres_537233245.webp "Building an Expressive AI on the Reachy Mini")

After a short break, [Sambhav Dalal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sambhav-dalal/) presented *MCP in Late 2026: Building AI Tools in Python That Hold Up in Production*, sharing practical lessons on building Model Context Protocol tools in Python that stay reliable beyond the demo.

![MCP in Late 2026](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/c/f/f/b/highres_537233243.webp "MCP in Late 2026")

Up next, [Manya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mkumar1205/) and [Neel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/engineel/) took the stage with *Lang2Sim: Natural Language to Executable Robot Simulation with Dynamic Stress Testing*, showing how plain-English descriptions can be turned into runnable robot simulations and then stress-tested automatically.

![Lang2Sim](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/d/0/0/4/highres_537233252.webp "Lang2Sim")

We also had a lightning talk:

[Shreya Kumar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alt-shreya/) delivered a lightning talk, *In memoriam: Tim Peters*, a tribute to one of Python's earliest core developers — the author of *The Zen of Python* (`import this`) and the creator of Timsort, the sorting algorithm behind Python's `sorted()` and `list.sort()`.

![In memoriam: Tim Peters](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/c/f/f/8/highres_537233240.webp "In memoriam: Tim Peters")
