The June BangPypers meetup was a fantastic event, hosted at [Red Hat](https://www.linkedin.com/company/red-hat/), where we co-hosted with [PyTorch](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pytorch/) and had insightful sessions covering PyTorch under the hood, hardware accelerators, video diffusion, and low-latency ML inference systems.

[Sumantro Mukherjee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sumantrom/) and [Arkadip Maitra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arkadip-maitra/) started the proceedings with their talk on *Introduction to Pytorch under the hood and ecosystem projects of foundation*. They explored the inner workings of PyTorch and gave a comprehensive overview of the foundation's surrounding ecosystem projects.

![Introduction to Pytorch under the hood](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/d/5/d/6/highres_534834742.webp "Introduction to Pytorch under the hood")

[Jewel Muraledhram](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jewelmuraledharan/) presented *Understanding PyTorch from ROCm's perspective: An accelerator PoV*. He offered a unique look into hardware acceleration, breaking down how PyTorch operates on the ROCm software stack.

![Understanding PyTorch from ROCm's perspective](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/1/7/3/e/highres_534905950.webp "Understanding PyTorch from ROCm's perspective")

[Joydeep Bhattacharjee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joydeep-bhattacharjee-934a1157/) then took the stage to discuss *Accelerating Video Diffusion for Inference*. He explained the optimizations and techniques necessary to run heavy video diffusion models efficiently in production environments.

![Accelerating Video Diffusion for Inference](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/d/5/d/0/highres_534834736.webp "Accelerating Video Diffusion for Inference")

[Shourya Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shourya-gupta-80237b1b9/) wrapped up the main talks with *Engineering Low-Latency ML Inference Systems with FastAPI and PyTorch*. He walked through the practical architecture and engineering practices required to build high-speed inference APIs.

![Engineering Low-Latency ML Inference Systems](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/c/b/f/d/highres_534832221.webp "Engineering Low-Latency ML Inference Systems")

We also had two engaging lightning talks:

[Shubhang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shubhangsinha/) delivered a lightning talk on *DNA Foundational Models*, providing a quick dive into how foundational models are being applied to genomic sequences.


[Abhik](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abhiksark/) presented a lightning talk introducing *[Pythonlings](https://pythonlings.abhik.ai/)*, giving the audience a great overview of this new resource.
