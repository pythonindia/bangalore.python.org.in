The **September BangPypers** meetup was an amazing event, hosted at [Rippling](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rippling), where we collaborated with [Rust India community](hhttps://www.linkedin.com/company/rust-india/) and had insightful sessions covering Python and Rust, core concepts like kernals and unicode texts were explored.

[Ishaan Arora](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pulsar17) started the proceedings with their talk on *Having fun with text in Python or 🐍❤🔠*. It deep dived into how text of different languages and emoji are rendered on screen and all the details it covers underneath them.

![IHaving fun with text in Python](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/b/2/2/d/highres_536205613.webp "Having fun with text in Python")

Then [Kushal Mittal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kushalmittal8/) presented *PyArrow inside an AI Agent: When a dataframe becomes an API*. He bought an interesting prespective of using dataframe mapping to call AI tools and why JSON is not optimal for all our use cases.

![PyArrow inside an AI Agent](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/b/2/1/f/highres_536205599.webp "PyArrow inside an AI Agent")

[Nihal Pasham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nihalpasham/) took then presenting *cuda-oxide: write CUDA (SIMT) kernels in pure Rust*. He explained how to write a CUDA (SIMT) kernels and also showed a live demo. Explained how magic of Rust is powering the new AI infrastructure.

![write CUDA (SIMT) kernels in pure Rust](https://secure.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/b/2/3/5/highres_536205621.webp "write CUDA (SIMT) kernels in pure Rust")


We also had an engaging lightning talks:

[Bhavya Bhatt](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bhavyabhatt/) delivered a lightning talk on *IDE friendly compiler construction - Jarvil*
