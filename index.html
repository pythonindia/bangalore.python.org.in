Take part

BangPypers is run by volunteers. Every meetup depends on people who come along, give talks and open up their offices.

  • Attend a meetup

    Meetups are always free. RSVP on Meetup or Luma so the hosts know how many people to expect. If Meetup asks you to pay, choose “Continue without paying”.

    RSVP on Meetup

  • Give a talk

    Open an issue in the meetup-talks repository describing your idea, for a meetup or an online Paper-n-PiP session. Organisers follow up to schedule it.

    Propose a talk

  • Host a meetup

    Companies across Bengaluru host us. If your office has room for a group on a Saturday, get in touch.

    Message the organisers

  • Keep the conversation going

    Ask questions, share projects and find collaborators between meetups.

    Join the Discord
