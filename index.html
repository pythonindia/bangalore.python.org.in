Take part

BangPypers is run by volunteers. Every meetup depends on people who come along, give talks and open up their offices.

  • Attend a meetup

    Meetups are free. RSVP on Meetup or Luma so the hosts know how many people to expect.

    RSVP on Meetup

  • Give a talk

    Open an issue in the meetup-talks repository describing your idea. Organisers follow up to schedule it.

    Propose a talk

  • Host a meetup

    Companies across Bengaluru host us. If your office has room for a group on a Saturday, get in touch.

    Message the organisers

  • Keep the conversation going

    Ask questions, share projects and find collaborators between meetups.

    Join the Discord
